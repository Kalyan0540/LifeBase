# App Ideas

Loose app ideas that may be useful to revisit later.

## Simple Expense Tracker

Problem: people struggle to keep tracking expenses because the normal flow has too much friction.

Core idea: detect outgoing payments from SMS or email notifications, show the amount immediately, and let the user log the category with one tap.

The product should stay intentionally simple:

- fast category tap after a payment
- monthly total spending
- spending by category
- basic spending patterns

The point is not a full finance dashboard. The point is making expense logging easy enough that people keep doing it.

## Learning Archive for WhatsApp Communities

Problem: many learning communities run on WhatsApp, but WhatsApp is poor for structured learning. Important lesson material gets buried under regular messages, greetings, links, voice notes, PDFs, and discussions.

Primary audience: elderly users in spiritual, devotional, or religious study groups such as Bhagavad Gita classes.

Secondary audience: community-led learning groups such as stitching, cooking, language, hobby, and workshop groups.

Core value proposition:

> Turn noisy WhatsApp groups into structured learning libraries.

Possible MVPs:

- Admin-curated library: admins forward important content into the app, where it gets organised into lessons and topics.
- AI-assisted organisation: users connect WhatsApp exports, and AI identifies lessons, topics, voice notes, images, and resources.

The strongest positioning may be as a knowledge extraction and organisation layer for existing community chats, not as a new learning platform.
