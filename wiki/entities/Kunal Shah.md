# Kunal Shah

Indian entrepreneur and product thinker. Co-founded FreeCharge (2010, acquired by Snapdeal 2015) and founded CRED (2018), a high-credit-score member platform. Known for the Delta 4 Framework — a model for predicting which products will grow organically vs. require sustained advertising.

## Key contributions in this wiki

- **[[Delta 4 Framework]]** — efficiency-delta scoring system: when the gap between old and new behaviour exceeds 4 (on a 10-point scale), a product creates irreversible behaviour, high user tolerance, and a Unique Brag-worthy Proposition (UBP) that drives organic growth

## Core ideas

Humans are uniquely and continuously efficiency-seeking — the entire product economy exists to serve this drive. Products that achieve Delta 4 spread through UBP (users gain social currency from sharing the discovery). Products below Delta 4 require ads and discounts to artificially inflate their perceived delta. The CRED growth story is itself a live proof of the framework.
