# Thomas Seyfried

Thomas Seyfried is a biology professor at Boston College and author of *Cancer as a Metabolic Disease*. In this source, he argues that cancer is fundamentally a mitochondrial metabolic disease and that treatment should target the glucose and glutamine metabolism of tumor cells.

## Source stance

The interview presents Seyfried as a researcher advocating for metabolic oncology, therapeutic ketosis, press-pulse cancer management, and more patient access to non-toxic metabolic strategies alongside standard treatments.

The medical claims should be interpreted as Seyfried's position unless separately supported by clinical guidelines.
