# Douglas Adams

British author (1952–2001). Best known for *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* and the Dirk Gently series. Also a technology enthusiast and early advocate for digital culture, who gave keynote talks at tech conferences including Microsoft's PDC.

## Key contributions in this wiki

- **[[PDC 1996 Keynote with Douglas Adams]]** — a philosophical meditation on how humans model reality, illustrated through experiments in perception, and the famous biscuit story

## Core ideas in this wiki

Adams's keynote argues that humans never perceive the world directly — we maintain a mental model and use incoming data to update it rather than perceive reality itself. We ignore most data (the optic nerve blind spot; the dolphin calibrating human hearing; the eyeball experiment substituting Hamlet for gibberish). We are so committed to our models that contradicting evidence is assimilated rather than corrected, as the biscuit story shows.

This connects to [[First Principles Thinking]]: the discipline of surfacing and questioning the model itself rather than reasoning within it. Adams makes the same epistemological point through comedy and storytelling that Elon Musk makes through engineering.
