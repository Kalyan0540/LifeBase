# Jared Spool

UX researcher, writer, and founder of User Interface Engineering (UIE). One of the most influential voices in UX strategy and design leadership. Known for evidence-based thinking, long-running user research, and frameworks that bridge design practice with business outcomes.

## Key contributions in this wiki

- **[[Kano Model]]** — popularised Noriaki Kano's satisfaction/investment model in the UX context; uses it to distinguish between basic expectations, performance payoff, and excitement generators as a strategic framework for design investment
- **[[UX Design Maturity Model]]** — five-stage organisational model (Dark Ages → Spot UX → UX as a Service → Embedded UX → Infused UX) for understanding and advancing design capability within organisations
- **[[User Journey Mapping]]** — maps all customer touchpoints on a frustration-to-delight scale; foundational tool used with both the Kano Model and the maturity model

## Core ideas

The move from "not frustrating" to "delightful" requires fundamentally different approaches (subtractive vs. additive design). Satisfaction is the absence of frustration — not the same as delight. Organisational design maturity is set by the least mature influencer on a team, not the most skilled. Every influencer needs immersive exposure to real users (≥2 hours per 6 weeks) to build design literacy.
