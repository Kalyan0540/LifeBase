# Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction

A Microsoft Research project page for the **Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction** — a set of 18 design best practices that synthesize 20+ years of human-AI interaction research. They were developed by Aether, Microsoft Research, and Office, and validated through a rigorous four-step process documented in the CHI 2019 paper of the same name.

The guidelines are organised by *when* they apply during a person's interaction with an AI system: initially, during regular interaction, when the system is inevitably wrong, and over time. They are intended as a tool throughout the design process — to evaluate existing ideas, brainstorm new ones, and align the multiple disciplines involved in building AI.

The page itself is brief (mostly a pointer to the summary poster and the CHI paper); the substance of the 18 guidelines is captured on the concept page.
