# Kunal Shah at Tech Sparks 2016

[[Kunal Shah]]'s talk at YourStory Tech Sparks 2016, delivered primarily in Hindi with English interspersed. Presents the [[Delta 4 Framework]] to a startup founder audience. Delivered when Shah was CEO of Freecharge (before founding CRED).

## Content

Covers the same core Delta 4 framework as the later [[Growth Without Ads- Is This The End Of The Ad World As We Know It? - Kunal Shah, Founder Cred|Growth Without Ads - Kunal Shah, Founder CRED]] talk:

- Human beings are unique in constantly seeking efficiency — the entire product economy flows from this
- When the delta between old and new behavioural states is ≥ 4 (on a 10-point scale), three things happen: irreversible behaviour, high tolerance, and Unique Brag-worthy Proposition (UBP)
- Below Delta 4: behaviour is reversible, tolerance is low, no UBP → requires advertising and discounting
- Framework applies to both B2C and B2B products

## Language note

The transcript is predominantly Hindi (Devanagari script) with code-switching to English. The core conceptual content is identical to the CRED talk. The audience here is explicitly startup founders, with Kunal directly applying the framework to help founders assess whether their product is achieving genuine Delta 4 or relying on artificial stimulus.

## Historical context

This is an earlier articulation of the Delta 4 model — Shah was still at Freecharge at the time. The CRED talk represents a more polished, English-language version of the same framework, enriched with CRED-specific examples.
