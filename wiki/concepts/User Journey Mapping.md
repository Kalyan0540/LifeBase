# User Journey Mapping

A UX tool for visualising the end-to-end experience of a customer across all touchpoints with a product or service. Associated with [[Jared Spool]]'s practice; used as a foundation for both the [[Kano Model]] and the [[UX Design Maturity Model]].

## What it is

Map every moment a customer has with your product or service — online and offline — on a scale from **extreme frustration** to **extreme delight**. The result is a curve showing where the experience rises and falls. From this map you can see:

- Which touchpoints are causing frustration (priority targets for removal)
- Which touchpoints are currently neutral (opportunities for excitement generators)
- Which touchpoints are already delightful (understand why; protect them)

## Why it matters

The Hyatt Hotel case study: random acts of generosity (excitement generators) failed to compensate for broken air conditioning and sloppy room service (failed basic expectations). Without a journey map, the CEO couldn't see that he was adding delight at one point on the curve while ignoring catastrophic frustration at other points. The map makes the tradeoffs visible.

## Connection to the Kano Model

Journey mapping produces the raw data that the [[Kano Model]] helps you analyse: which touchpoints are basic expectations, which are performance payoff, and which have excitement generator potential?

## Connection to experience vision

In [[Jared Spool – Beyond The UX Tipping Point|the maturity talk]], Spool describes a **shared experience vision** as the aspirational version of the journey map: "what would it mean to make this entire journey delightful?" You tell that story, and it becomes the flag everyone marches toward.

## Immersive exposure as the input

The best journey maps are built from direct user observation. Spool's "immersive exposure" play — 2 hours every 6 weeks per influencer watching real users — generates the raw material for an accurate journey map. Self-reported satisfaction surveys miss the frustrations users don't consciously notice.
