# App Store Optimization

App Store Optimization (ASO) is the practice of improving an app’s visibility in store search results and increasing the share of listing visitors who install it.

## Two parts of the system

1. **Discovery** — help Apple or Google match the app to valuable searches.
2. **Conversion** — make the listing clear and credible enough that visitors download it.

Strong discovery without conversion wastes traffic. Strong conversion without discovery leaves the app unseen.

## Three foundations

### 1. Keyword placement

- Research search terms, then validate their popularity and difficulty.
- Put the primary keyword in the app name.
- Use the subtitle for secondary terms without repeating name keywords.
- Use the hidden keyword field efficiently without spaces, duplicates, unnecessary plurals, or competitor names.
- Optimise for valuable demand, not merely an easy ranking.

### 2. Screenshot conversion

- Lead with the most distinctive and valuable product capability.
- Show real UI and make the product understandable quickly.
- Avoid generic onboarding screens and decorative imagery that does not explain value.
- A/B test listing variants. Visual polish is not evidence of better conversion.

### 3. Ratings and reviews

- Ask after a meaningful success, not during onboarding or after a failure.
- Respect dismissals and avoid repeated prompts.
- Respond to reviews and resolve reported problems.
- Ask gently after successful support interactions.
- Use repeated review themes as product feedback.

## Operating model

ASO is an iterative loop:

1. Research and update metadata.
2. Test listing creative.
3. Improve the product and review experience.
4. Track search position and conversion.
5. Ship updates and repeat.

Rankings, review volume, and conversion can reinforce each other, but the gains may take months or years to compound.
