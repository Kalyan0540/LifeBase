# Delta 4 Framework

A product evaluation framework developed by [[Kunal Shah]] for predicting whether a product will spread organically or require sustained advertising and discounting. Based on the observation that humans are uniquely and continuously efficiency-seeking — the entire product economy exists to satisfy this drive.

## The scoring mechanism

Rate the old behaviour (the status quo) and the new behaviour (your product) each out of 10. Calculate the delta.

**When delta ≥ 4:**
1. **Irreversible behaviour** — users will not return to the old way. The new behaviour becomes default.
2. **High tolerance** — users tolerate glitches, outages, and gaps rather than switching back (e.g., persisting with Ola even when maps fail).
3. **UBP (Unique Brag-worthy Proposition)** — users spontaneously tell others. Discovery feels like a magic trick. Social currency accrues to the sharer. The product spreads as peer-to-peer UBP.

**When delta < 4:**
- Behaviour is reversible after first bad experience
- Zero tolerance — users switch at the first inconvenience
- No UBP — no one tells anyone
- Requires heavy advertising, discounts, and cashbacks to artificially inflate perceived delta

## Worked examples

| Old behaviour | New behaviour | Delta | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hailing a taxi | Uber/Ola | ~5 | Irreversible, UBP, viral |
| Buying shirts offline | Online clothing | ~−1 to 1 | Reversible, requires discounts |
| Calling a restaurant | Food delivery app (urban power user) | ~4 | Spreads within segment |

## The ad spend diagnostic

If a company requires massive ad spend or cashbacks to grow, it almost always signals sub-Delta 4 efficiency gain. True Delta 4 products spread on UBP alone. TrueCaller spread because early users could show it as a magic trick — look up any unknown number's owner — and the social currency of that trick was irresistible.

## Relationship to other frameworks

- **[[Hook Model]]**: both create irreversible behaviour, but through different mechanisms. Delta 4 is about the efficiency gain being so large the old behaviour becomes unthinkable. Hook Model is about emotional associations that fire automatically.
- **[[Flywheel Effect]]**: Delta 4 products exhibit flywheel dynamics — the UBP spreads users → more users → more data → better product → stronger UBP.
- **[[The MAYA Principle]]**: Delta 4 measures the efficiency jump; MAYA measures the acceptability ceiling. A product could be Delta 6 in raw efficiency terms but still fail if the new behaviour is too unfamiliar (above the MAYA threshold).
