# Human-AI Interaction Guidelines

A set of 18 research-validated design guidelines (Microsoft / Aether, CHI 2019) for how AI systems should behave toward people. They are organised by the phase of interaction in which they apply.

## Initially

- **G1 — Make clear what the system can do.** Help the user understand its capabilities.
- **G2 — Make clear how well the system can do what it can do.** Help the user understand how often it may make mistakes.

## During interaction

- **G3 — Time services based on context.** Act or interrupt based on the user's current task and environment.
- **G4 — Show contextually relevant information.** Display information relevant to the user's current task and context.
- **G5 — Match relevant social norms.** Deliver the experience in a way users expect given their social and cultural context.
- **G6 — Mitigate social biases.** Ensure language and behaviour don't reinforce unfair stereotypes and biases.

## When wrong

- **G7 — Support efficient invocation.** Make it easy to invoke or request the AI's services when needed.
- **G8 — Support efficient dismissal.** Make it easy to dismiss or ignore unwanted services.
- **G9 — Support efficient correction.** Make it easy to edit, refine, or recover when the system is wrong.
- **G10 — Scope services when in doubt.** Disambiguate or gracefully degrade when uncertain about the user's goal.
- **G11 — Make clear why the system did what it did.** Enable access to an explanation of the system's behaviour.

## Over time

- **G12 — Remember recent interactions.** Maintain short-term memory and let the user reference it.
- **G13 — Learn from user behaviour.** Personalise by learning from the user's actions over time.
- **G14 — Update and adapt cautiously.** Limit disruptive changes when updating and adapting behaviour.
- **G15 — Encourage granular feedback.** Let users give feedback on their preferences during regular interaction.
- **G16 — Convey the consequences of user actions.** Make clear how user actions will affect future behaviour.
- **G17 — Provide global controls.** Let users globally customise what the system monitors and how it behaves.
- **G18 — Notify users about changes.** Inform users when the system adds or updates capabilities.

## Why it matters

The guidelines give a shared, phase-based checklist for evaluating and brainstorming AI experiences. The applied generative-AI article [[Creating a Dynamic UX - Guidance for Generative AI Applications]] maps its lifecycle advice (first run, during interaction, when wrong, over time) directly onto these four phases.
