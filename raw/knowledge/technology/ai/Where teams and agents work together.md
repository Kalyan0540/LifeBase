## Airplane and rocket analogy

Imagine your goal is to reach the moon. In the olden days before the invention of rockets, people used the think of existing solutions and improve them to reach the moon. In their case, it’s the airplane.

They started to build airplanes with heavy motors which indeed improved the altitude and speed they used to fly from their previous models. Slowly and slowly they have understood that the speed at which the plane is taking over is not enough to surpass gravity and go to the moon.
