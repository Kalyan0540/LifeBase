# Expense Tracking App – Raw Idea

## Background

I’ve noticed that a lot of people struggle to consistently track their expenses.

There are already many expense-tracking apps in the market, ranging from highly automated solutions to completely manual ones.

Some apps automatically categorize expenses by reading SMS messages and identifying details such as the merchant, amount, and transaction type. Apps like these reduce manual effort by understanding spending patterns directly from transaction notifications.

On the other hand, there are manual expense-tracking apps. Personally, I use an app called MyMoney, where I manually enter every expense and income. It supports budgets, categories, notes, and even has an inbuilt calculator. While it is useful, it still requires regular manual effort.

## Observation

Despite the availability of these apps, many people either:

- Never start tracking expenses.
    
- Stop using the app after some time.
    
- Find the process too complicated or time-consuming.
    

The friction of opening an app, selecting categories, entering amounts, and saving transactions often becomes a barrier.

## Core Thought

What if expense tracking was reduced to a single tap?

Whenever a payment is made, users typically receive:

- An SMS notification.
    
- An email notification.
    
- Or both.
    

The idea is to detect outgoing payment transactions and immediately present a very simple interface to the user.

## User Experience

When a payment notification is received:

1. The app identifies the transaction amount.
    
2. A notification or full-screen view appears.
    
3. The amount is prominently displayed.
    
4. A suggested category is shown in a large, easy-to-tap format.
    
5. The user simply taps the category.
    
6. The expense is recorded instantly.
    

The interaction should take only a few seconds.

## Philosophy

The goal is not to build a feature-rich finance management tool.

The goal is to make expense tracking so simple that people actually continue using it.

## Output

At the end of the month, users can view:

- Total spending.
    
- Spending by category.
    
- Basic spending patterns.
    

Nothing more complex than that.

The focus is simplicity, speed, and reducing the effort required to log expenses.
